[h1]ProgressionLESS: Kitchen[/h1]
[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3424640315]Progression: Kitchen[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Integrates food into the grander RimWorld ecosystem, with cooking that unlocks through its tech eras.
[*]Cooking starts with simple meals and a fueled stove, then climbs through fine, lavish and gourmet meals.
[*]Brings cooking, brewing and food mods into the same progression, with research names that match the stations they unlock.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Kitchen adds up to [b]47 research projects[/b] depending on your food mods. This fork cuts that to [b]at most 7[/b], and keeps ferny's meal tiers:
[list]
[*][b]Simple meals[/b], [b]Fueled stove[/b], [b]Fine meals[/b], [b]Lavish meals[/b] and [b]Butcher table[/b] (Neolithic)
[*][b]Gourmet meals[/b] (Neolithic, with Vanilla Cooking Expanded)
[*][b]Chemfuel stove[/b] (Industrial, with Appliances Expanded and Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded)
[/list]
Everything else unlocks with the research it already builds on:
[list]
[*]Ales, ciders, wines, mead and other drinks: [b]Brewing[/b]
[*]Packaged rations: [b]Pemmican[/b]
[*]Condiments: [b]Vanilla Cooking Expanded's condiments research[/b]
[*]Bakes and desserts: the matching meal tier
[*]Flour and sugar milling: [b]Simple meals[/b]
[*]Electric stove, electric butcher and electric cheese press: [b]Electricity[/b]
[*]Large stoves: [b]Fabrication[/b]
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Vanilla Cooking Expanded, plus Stews, Sushi and Haute, and the carnivore/vegetarian meals patch
[*]Vanilla Brewing Expanded, plus Coffees and Teas
[*]Vanilla Plants Expanded and More Plants
[*]Vanilla Factions Expanded: Tribals, Classical, Medieval 2 and Production
[*]Medieval Overhaul and House Roxmont
[*]Appliances Expanded and Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded
[*]Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded and Helixien Gas Production
[*]Industrial Hardleather Concrete and Cheese
[*]Stoneborn Delights, Keshig, Dark Ages Beasts and Monsters, and Mythic Ages Megafauna Bestiary
[*]Alpha Crafts, Ponpeco Pajamas, Medicines Plus and Meat on a Stick
[*]Processor Framework, Biotech and Node Research
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]
