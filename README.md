# ProgressionLESS: Kitchen

**All the Progression goodness, less research.**

Integrates food into the grander RimWorld ecosystem, with cooking that unlocks through its tech eras.

The "LESS" is less research: Progression: Kitchen adds up to 47 research projects depending on your cooking mods. This fork cuts that to at most 7, and keeps ferny's meal tiers: simple meals, fueled stove, fine meals, lavish meals and gourmet meals. Drinks, rations, condiments, bakes, desserts and appliances unlock with the research they already build on, such as brewing, pemmican, electricity and fabrication. Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.

Forked from [Progression: Kitchen](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3424640315) by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [The Progression Modpack](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585).
